FAQ

Good to know.

How do I order?

Add items to your bag and tap Checkout on WhatsApp. Your order is sent to us as a message so we can confirm availability.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa or cash on delivery — we confirm the details with you on WhatsApp.

Can I order custom or bulk clothing?

Yes. Use the quote form in the Custom section with your quantity, logo and deadline.

How do accounts work?

An account saves your details, favourites and order history on the device you use. It makes checkout faster, and you can still order without one.
